The story of Guitar Struck

A sonic journey that begins with Guitatrain, evoking the rhythm of a train heading toward new horizons. Guitar East transports listeners to an exotic landscape rich in culture and tradition. Energy surges through Guitar Struck, the rhythm turns celebratory in Guitars Dance, and the adventure ends with two guitars sharing their story in Guitars Talk.
